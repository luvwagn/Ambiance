Pictures
--------
Ambiance's JPEG decoder, jdec (src/jdec/jdec.c), was written for
Ambiance and is under Ambiance's MIT licence; it contains no code from
libjpeg or libjpeg-turbo. It follows the methods they use and document,
so that its pictures match theirs: the Arai-Agui-Nakajima fast inverse
DCT (as in the Independent JPEG Group's jidctfst.c), libjpeg's "fancy"
triangle-filter upsampling of 4:2:2 and 4:2:0 chroma, and its YCbCr to
RGB conversion and rounding (JFIF, ITU-T T.871). It is tested against
libjpeg (through Pillow) at every scale. With thanks to the Independent
JPEG Group (libjpeg, (c) 1991-2020 Thomas G. Lane, Guido Vollbeding) and
the libjpeg-turbo Project.

are Mozilla's (its CA certificate list, as certifi packages it), under
the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (see Licences/Mozilla-CA-certificates.txt;

HTTPS (secure radio stations, signing in at plex.tv) is by AmiSSL 5
(AmiSSL Open Source Team, Apache License 2.0; OpenSSL), where it is
installed. It is not part of Ambiance or its archive: get it from Aminet
(util/libs/AmiSSL-5.x-OS3) or https://github.com/jens-maus/amissl.

Packed modules are unpacked with xfdmaster.library, MP3 is decoded by
mpega.library and XM, S3M and IT are mixed by modplug.library, where they
are installed. They are not part of Ambiance. Its archive's Extras drawer
holds their Aminet archives whole and unchanged, which the installer
offers to install where they are missing or older (never on AGS):
  modplug-68k: modplug.library 1.0 by Thomas Wenzel, a port of
    libmodplug 0.8.9, which is in the public domain.
  mpega_library: mpega.library 2.4, (c) 1997-99 Stephane Tavenard,
    giftware: "may be distributed freely under the condition that no
    profit is gained from its distribution, nor from any other program
    distributed in the same package", all files together and unchanged.
  xfdmaster: xfdmaster 1.38 (library 39.15), (c) Georg Hoermann and Dirk
    Stoecker: "freely distributable for noncommercial use. If you
    redistribute XFD, you have to keep all files together."
So Ambiance's archive, with these in it, is not to be sold.

Libs/amcesql.library, which reads beets music libraries, is Ambiance's own
small wrapper (src/sqlib, MIT) around SQLite 3.34.0, linked from the
libsqlite3.a of Steffen Gutmann's AmigaOS 3 port (Aminet: biz/dbase/
sqlite-3.34.0.a-amiga). SQLite, by D. Richard Hipp and others, is in the
public domain (Licences/SQLite.txt). Ambiance opens the library only when
a beets library is set up.

Libs/dvdnav.library, which reads DVDs, is libdvdread 7.1.1 and libdvdnav
7.0.0 (VideoLAN, from the Ogle DVD player: Daniel Caujolle-Bert, Rich
Wareham, Michael Roitzsch, Jean-Baptiste Kempf, Erik Hovland and others;
their AUTHORS files are in its source) built as an Exec library, with
three lines changed for AmigaOS. They are under the GNU General Public
License, version 2 or later, and the library under version 3 or later
(Licences/dvdnav.library.txt); its source is at
https://github.com/luvwagn/dvdnav.library (extras/dvdnav in Ambiance's
source). Ambiance opens it at run time, through its MIT interface, only
when a DVD is played; none of its code is in Ambiance. It was called
amcedvd.library before 0.9.1.1.

Libs/ac3.library and Libs/ac3fpu.library, which decode AC-3 (Dolby
Digital) sound, are liba52 0.8.0 (Aaron Holtzman, Michel Lespinasse and
others; VideoLAN; its AUTHORS file is in its source) built as Exec
libraries: ac3.library its integer build, ac3fpu.library its floating-
point build for a 68040 or 68060's FPU. The libraries are their own
project (extras/ac3, https://github.com/luvwagn/ac3.library): liba52 is
under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later, the libraries
version 3 or later (Licences/ac3.library.txt), their source at that
address, not in this archive (what was changed in liba52:
third_party/liba52/CHANGES there). Their interface (include/,
ac3_lib.sfd, ac3.doc) is MIT, for any program. Ambiance opens one at run
time, through that interface, only for a video's AC3 the ZZ9000 will not
decode; none of their code is in Ambiance.

FLAC (Plex tracks as stored, and the files in Music folders) is decoded by
flacdec.library where it is installed; it is not included in the archive.
flacdec.library is (c) 2026 Renaud Schweingruber, "free to use and to
redistribute in this unchanged archive" (Aminet: util/libs/flacdec). It
contains dr_flac by David Reid, public domain or MIT-0
(https://github.com/mackron/dr_libs). Ambiance uses only its published
interface (five functions) and carries none of its files.

Radio's station lists come from radio-browser.info, a free directory of
internet radio stations kept by its community (https://www.radio-browser.info).

Libs/amcempega.library, which decodes MP3 (and MPEG audio's Layers I and

Underbit Technologies; with Debian's security fixes) behind mpega.library's

Public License, version 2 or later, the library version 3 or later

Video
-----
MPEG-1 and MPEG-2 video without a ZZ9000 (or with its decoding turned off)
is decoded by Libs/mpegv.library 2, its own project (extras/mpegv,
https://github.com/luvwagn/mpegv.library), GPL-3.0-or-later
(Licences/mpegv.library.txt), its source at that address, not in this
archive: libmpeg2 0.5.1, by Aaron Holtzman, Michel Lespinasse and others
(GPL-2.0-or-later), made faster for a 68k (its CHANGES says how), its
pictures the same. Ambiance opens it at run time. Its sound is decoded by
mpega.library, or a DVD's AC-3 by ac3.library.

On a ZZ9000, MPEG video is decoded by the card through zz9k.library,
called with the ZZ9000 SDK's headers (BlitterStudio's zz9000-firmware,
MIT; zz9k.library itself GPL-3.0-or-later, with a section 7 permission for
programs that call it through those headers): third_party/zz9k-sdk.txt.
