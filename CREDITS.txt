(c) The Pixelify Sans Project Authors, licensed under the SIL Open Font

Galmuri11, Galmuri11 Bold and Galmuri14, (c) 2019-2025 Lee Minseo,
licensed under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 (see OFL-Galmuri.txt).
amce-galmuri14b is Galmuri14 thickened by a pixel. The converted fonts

has not, Ark Pixel Font 12px, (c) 2021 TakWolf, licensed under the SIL
Open Font Licence 1.1 (see OFL-ArkPixel.txt). The glyph packs in

Packed modules are unpacked with xfdmaster.library, where it is installed.

MP3 is decoded by mpega.library, and FLAC (Plex tracks as stored, and the
files in Music folders) by flacdec.library, where they are installed; they
are not part of AmigaMCE and are not included in its archive.
flacdec.library is (c) 2026 Renaud Schweingruber, "free to use and to
redistribute in this unchanged archive" (Aminet: util/libs/flacdec). It
contains dr_flac by David Reid, public domain or MIT-0
(https://github.com/mackron/dr_libs). AmigaMCE uses only its published
interface (five functions) and carries none of its files.

Radio's station lists come from radio-browser.info, a free directory of
internet radio stations kept by its community (https://www.radio-browser.info).
