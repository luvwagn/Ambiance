Ambiance's Visuals drawer
=========================

Put AmigaAMP's visual plug-ins here (or in AmigaAMP:Plugins). Choose one
in Settings > Display > Full screen visual, then press F on Now Playing:
Ambiance starts it, answers on the port AmigaAMP's plug-ins look for
("AmigaAMP plugin port"), and gives it what plays: the sound, its
spectrum, the title. Ambiance's screen goes behind while it runs; Esc in
the plug-in (or the music ending) brings Ambiance back.

Most plug-ins open their window on Workbench (the default public
screen), so they want what that screen can show: a graphics card's
screen for most, Warp3D or a PowerPC for some. Plug-ins on Aminet:
mus/play (AmigaAMP-VisualPlugins, VisFS, PlasmaPlugin, BlurPlugin, ...).

Without one, F on Now Playing shows Flow, Ambiance's own.
